Antoine Griezmann is a French professional footballer born on March 21, 1991, in Mâcon, France. He is widely known for his creativity, vision, passing ability, and goal-scoring skills. Griezmann primarily plays as a forward or attacking midfielder.

He began his professional career with Real Sociedad before becoming a star at Atlético Madrid. He also played for FC Barcelona and has represented the France national football team in major international tournaments.

Griezmann played a key role in helping France win the 2018 FIFA World Cup and has earned recognition as one of the best footballers of his generation. His intelligence on the field, teamwork, and consistency have made him a fan favorite around the world.

Career Stats

Real Sociedad (2009–2014)

Griezmann began his professional career with Real Sociedad in Spain. He made his first-team debut in 2009 and helped the club earn promotion to La Liga. During his five seasons, he established himself as one of the league's most promising young forwards.

Atlético Madrid (2014–2019)

In 2014, Griezmann signed for Atlético Madrid. He quickly became the club's star player, known for his goal-scoring ability and creativity. He won the UEFA Europa League and UEFA Super Cup, while also helping Atlético reach major domestic and European finals.

FC Barcelona (2019–2021)

Griezmann joined FC Barcelona in 2019. During his time at Barcelona, he contributed important goals and assists and helped the club win the Copa del Rey in the 2020–21 season.

France National Team (2014–2024)

Griezmann represented France for a decade, making 137 appearances and scoring 44 goals. He helped the team win the 2018 FIFA World Cup and the 2021 UEFA Nations League before retiring from international football in 2024.

Orlando City (2026–Present)

In 2026, Griezmann joined Orlando City SC, beginning a new chapter in Major League Soccer. He brings his experience, creativity, and leadership to the club as one of its star players.

Legacy

Antoine Griezmann began his football journey in France before moving to Spain, where he started his professional career with Real Sociedad.
