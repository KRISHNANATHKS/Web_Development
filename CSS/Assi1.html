Harry Potter Series

Harry Potter and the Philosopher’s Stone (2001)


An orphaned boy discovers he is a wizard and enrolls in Hogwarts, where he learns the truth about his family and the dark wizard who killed them.

Harry Potter and the Chamber of Secrets (2002)


During his second year, a mysterious voice petrifies students, and Harry must uncover the legend of the Chamber of Secrets to save the school.

Harry Potter and the Prisoner of Azkaban (2004)


Harry’s third year is marked by the escape of Sirius Black, a dangerous prisoner believed to be hunting Harry, and the introduction of soul-sucking Dementors.
